### Private vs. federal student loans

Congress gave the CFPB statutory authority over private student loans, so our online resources are focused accordingly. Federal student loan programs, including federal loans, are within the sole legal purview of the Department of Education

## Getting a student loan

### Get started

It is generally recommended that undergraduate students exhaust their federal student loan options before considering a private student loan. If you know you are interested in a federal student loan, please visit the Department of Education’s website by clicking here

If you are not sure what type of loan you want you may have options around how you pay for your education. Understanding your choices can help you make the right decision for your situation. Read more
